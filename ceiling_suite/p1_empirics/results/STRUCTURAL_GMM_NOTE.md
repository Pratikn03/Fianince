# Note on `structural_gmm_calibration_results.json` (13 November 2026)

This calibration was added to `main` on 13 November 2026 by an AI tool and cited in the paper's scope section. It is
not registered and is not used in any version of the paper. Several estimates sit at what appear to be optimizer
bounds (q = -2.0, sigma_w^2 = 499.999), kappa has t = 0.05, and the sample is 3,364 five-minute bars (about 12 days)
of BTC-USDT and ETH-USDT whose source files are not in the repository. The sentence "the structural estimates confirm
highly persistent algorithmic memory" was removed. Under the project's rules, any empirical use of these data would
need a registered specification before the data are read.
