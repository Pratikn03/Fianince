# Co-author workbooks

- `Paper1_Questions_Workbook.docx` (version 2, 6 Nov 2026; built by `review/make_workbook_v2.py`) is the current workbook. Its questions follow the SSRN working paper's numbering and include Sujan Khatiwada's corrections of 6 Nov to the earlier version.
- The following files predate it and should not be used for study: `CoAuthor_Mastery_Workbook_Paper1.docx`, `CoAuthor_Mastery_Workbook_Paper1_Complete_With_Answers.docx` and `Paper1_Answers_Solution_Manual.docx`. They use a different model (inventory damping `q`, closed loop `G = κH/(1+κqH)`), wrong theorem numbers, and claims the paper does not make (an explosive root, a (2K×2K) Hankel system, "identical projector conditioning", "institutional suicide", a "40× flash crash", exact multinomial resampling at all N, proven within-class cancellation).
- The answer key is Sujan Khatiwada's "Answers to the 30 workbook questions" with his "Expanded explanations and recommended corrections" (6 Nov 2026).
