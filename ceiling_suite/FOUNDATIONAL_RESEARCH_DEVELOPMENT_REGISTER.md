### 1.1 Paper 1 — Identification Limits ([p1_mixture.tex](file:///Users/pratik_n/Downloads/Machine_Markets_Master_Release/ceiling_suite/paper1/p1_mixture.tex))
- **Location**: Proposition 6.4 (`prop:esprit`) and Theorem 6.3 (`thm:kcohort`).
- **Audit Finding**:
  1. Unordered cohort labels create a permutation ambiguity when defining rate recovery error $\max_i |\hat\eta_i - \eta_i|$.
  2. The minimax lower bound requires the parameter class $\Theta_K$ to contain the Gauss / Gauss-Radau moment-matching pair $(\eta, q)$ and $(\eta', q')$ while enforcing separation $\Delta\eta$ and weight floor $q_{\min}$ for the Prony/ESPRIT upper bound.
- **Resolution Implemented**:
  1. Defined the unordered cohort Mixing Measure $\mathcal M = \{(\eta_i, q_i) : i = 1, \dots, K\}$ and permutation-invariant matching metric
     $$d_{\mathrm{perm}}(\hat{\mathcal M}, \mathcal M) = \min_{\pi \in S_K} \max_{1 \le i \le K} |\hat\eta_{\pi(i)} - \eta_i|,$$
     which coincides with ordered recovery under the canonical ordering $\eta_1 < \eta_2 < \dots < \eta_K$.
  2. Explicitly defined the separated parameter class $\Theta_K(\Delta\eta, q_{\min}, \eta_{\min}, \eta_{\max})$ requiring $\min_{i \ne j}|\eta_i - \eta_j| \ge \Delta\eta$, $|q_i| \ge q_{\min} > 0$, $\eta_i \in [\eta_{\min}, \eta_{\max}] \subset (0,1)$, and verified that the shifted Legendre Gauss / Gauss-Radau quadrature nodes and weights on $[r_c - \tfrac{K}{2}\Delta\eta, r_c + \tfrac{K}{2}\Delta\eta]$ lie inside $\Theta_K$.
  3. Formalized in Lean 4: [`CurrentMM.p1_prop_esprit_minimax_upper`](file:///Users/pratik_n/Downloads/Machine_Markets_Master_Release/research_extension/current_manuscript_lean_20261008/CurrentProofs/CeilingSuitePaper1.lean#L430-L448) and [`MachineMarkets.p1_prop_esprit_minimax_upper`](file:///Users/pratik_n/Downloads/Machine_Markets_Master_Release/lean_proofs/Proofs/CeilingSuitePaper1.lean#L436-L454).

### 1.2 Paper 2 — Financial Stability and Control ([p2_theory2.tex](file:///Users/pratik_n/Downloads/Machine_Markets_Master_Release/ceiling_suite/paper2/p2_theory2.tex))
- **Location 1**: Section 5.1 Quantity Gate (`eq:gate`, [p2_theory2.tex](file:///Users/pratik_n/Downloads/Machine_Markets_Master_Release/ceiling_suite/paper2/p2_theory2.tex#L116-L125)).
  - **Audit Finding**: Using single-impulse peak gain $G_{\mathrm{single},t} = \max_{m<t}|g_m|$ for a per-period bounded trade sequence $|v_j| \le V$ understates cumulative worst-case exposure when multiple impulse lags are nonzero.
  - **Resolution Implemented**: Defined both the cumulative convolution gain $G_{\mathrm{cum},t} = \sum_{m=0}^{t-1}|g_m|$ and the single-impulse peak gain $G_{\mathrm{single},t} = \max_{m<t}|g_m|$, replacing the repeated-trade gate with
    $$V^*_{\mathrm{repeated}} = \min_{t:\,G_{\mathrm{cum},t}+c_{\mathrm{exec}}>0} \frac{B - L_{\mathrm{other}} - |b_t|}{G_{\mathrm{cum},t} + c_{\mathrm{exec}}}, \qquad \bar R = V^*_{\mathrm{repeated}}/\tau,$$
    alongside its single-shock counterpart $V^*_{\mathrm{single}}$, and proved $G_{\mathrm{cum},t} \ge G_{\mathrm{single},t}$ with strict inequality whenever two or more lags are nonzero.
- **Location 2**: Proposition 4.3 (`prop:shape`, [p2_theory2.tex](file:///Users/pratik_n/Downloads/Machine_Markets_Master_Release/ceiling_suite/paper2/p2_theory2.tex#L42-L54)).
  - **Audit Finding**: The continuous-relaxation formula $\tilde k = \frac{\log(A/\text{den})}{\log(a/b)}$ requires interior assumptions $a \in (0,1)$, $b \in (0,1)$, $a \ne b$, and $A > 0$.
  - **Resolution Implemented**: Explicitly stated interior conditions $a \in (0,1)$, $b = 1 - r \in (0,1)$, $a \ne b$, and $A = \varphi\varepsilon - (1-a)\kappa > 0$ for the logarithmic formula, and added complete closed-form boundary expressions for:
    - $a = 0$: $h_0 = \kappa$, $h_k = \varphi\varepsilon b^{k-1}$ ($k \ge 1$), peaking at $k^* = 1$ when $\varphi\varepsilon > \kappa$;
    - $a = b$: confluent limit $h_k = \kappa a^k + k\varphi\varepsilon a^{k-1}$, peaking near $\tilde k = 1 - \frac{\kappa a}{\varphi\varepsilon}\log(1/a)$ when $\varphi\varepsilon > (1-a)\kappa$;
    - $A \le 0$: monotone decay with $k^* = 0$ and $\max_{k \ge 0} h_k = \kappa$.
- **Location 3**: Proposition 5.6 (`prop:dominance`, [p2_theory2.tex](file:///Users/pratik_n/Downloads/Machine_Markets_Master_Release/ceiling_suite/paper2/p2_theory2.tex#L235-L246)).
  - **Audit Finding**: Distinguish worst-case breach over the Euclidean state ball $\{\tilde z_t : \|\tilde z_t\|_2 \le \|z_t\|_2\}$ from exact state-specific reachable breach at a single vector $z_t$.
  - **Resolution Implemented**: Split `prop:dominance` into part `(i)` (Euclidean state-ball norm bound $M_C(\alpha_\rho)\|z_t\|_2 + s(\alpha_\rho)|g_0(\alpha_\rho)|\bar u_{\mathrm{step},t}$) and part `(ii)` (exact state-specific reachable excursion $|c^\top_{\mathrm{price}}J(\alpha_\rho)z_t| + s(\alpha_\rho)|g_0(\alpha_\rho)|\bar u_{\mathrm{step},t}$), formalized in [`CurrentMM.p2_prop_dominance_guard_vs_eigenvalue`](file:///Users/pratik_n/Downloads/Machine_Markets_Master_Release/research_extension/current_manuscript_lean_20261008/CurrentProofs/CeilingSuitePaper2.lean#L516-L537).

All 34 computational and empirical artifacts across Paper 1 and Paper 2 are catalogued in [`ceiling_suite/EXPERIMENT_MANIFEST.csv`](file:///Users/pratik_n/Downloads/Machine_Markets_Master_Release/ceiling_suite/EXPERIMENT_MANIFEST.csv). Key reconciliations completed in Phase 3:

1. **$K$-Cohort Prony/ESPRIT Upper Bound for $K \in \{2, 3, 4, 5\}$ ([`kcohort_upper.py`](file:///Users/pratik_n/Downloads/Machine_Markets_Master_Release/ceiling_suite/theory/kcohort_upper.py))**:

## PHASE 7 — Unified Foundational Framework (Paper 1 $\leftrightarrow$ Paper 2 Bridge)

Formalized in [`CurrentProofs/CeilingSuitePaper2.lean`](file:///Users/pratik_n/Downloads/Machine_Markets_Master_Release/research_extension/current_manuscript_lean_20261008/CurrentProofs/CeilingSuitePaper2.lean#L597-L639) and [`Proofs/CeilingSuitePaper2.lean`](file:///Users/pratik_n/Downloads/Machine_Markets_Master_Release/lean_proofs/Proofs/CeilingSuitePaper2.lean#L594-L636), and integrated into Section 7 ([p2_close.tex](file:///Users/pratik_n/Downloads/Machine_Markets_Master_Release/ceiling_suite/paper2/p2_close.tex#L9)):

1. **Foundational Result A (`foundational_result_A_impossibility`) — Impossibility of Cohort-Specific Risk Control from Public Data**:
   - Any risk constraint depending on individual cohort memory parameters $(\eta_i, q_i)$ separately from the aggregate impulse response $(h_k)_{k \ge 0}$ takes distinct values on observationally equivalent cohort economies that share the same merged measure $\mathcal M$ and identical worst-case loss $L_{\max} = |I|\bar u \sum_{k < H}|h_k|$. Conversely, the summed-gain guard depends only on $\sum_{k<H}|h_k|$ and is invariant across the entire observational equivalence class.
2. **Foundational Result B (`foundational_result_B_disclosure_control`) — Disclosure-Enabled Control Improvement**:
   - Under operator estimation uncertainty $\Delta G(\tau)$, the robust admission rule satisfies $I_t U (\hat G_H + \Delta G(\tau)) \le B_t$. Because class-tagged disclosure reduces the asymptotic sample complexity of cohort recovery by a factor of order $(\Delta\eta)^{-(4K-4)}$ (Paper 1 `prop:crb`), it shrinks the estimation confidence radius $\Delta G^{\mathrm{disc}}(\tau) < \Delta G^{\mathrm{pub}}(\tau)$, strictly enlarging admissible inventory $I_{\max}^{\mathrm{disc}} > I_{\max}^{\mathrm{pub}}$ without compromising forward solvency.
3. **Foundational Result C (`foundational_result_C_probing_control_tradeoff`) — Active Probing vs. Transient Excursion Tradeoff**:
   - Active probing trades $(u_t)$ of cumulative size $\|u\|_{\ell_1} > 0$ injected to shrink $\Delta G(\tau)$ without disclosure consume $\kappa\|u\|_{\ell_1}$ of the worst-case transient excursion budget, creating an unavoidable tradeoff $I_t U \Delta G(\tau) + \kappa\|u\|_{\ell_1}$ between identification uncertainty and probing-induced price dislocation.
