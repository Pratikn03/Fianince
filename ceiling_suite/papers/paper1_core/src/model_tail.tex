
\paragraph{Why exponential memory, and what is assumed.} Lemma~\ref{lem:micro} shows that two different design philosophies, Bayesian filtering and regularized online learning, produce the same reduced-form state. In these two families the memory rate is a known function of a tuning parameter, the desk's signal-to-noise ratio or its regularization. That is the private information a supervisor would like to recover, although real algorithms need not belong to either family. Applied to the pricing error, a desk that cannot separate fundamental news from transitory deviations treats recent deviations as a persistent trend, which is the exponential extrapolation of \citet{barberis2015,barberis2018}. Nothing in the model says such trading is profitable.

The results use six modelling choices: linearity; price-first timing within a period; exponential memory (needed for the identification result, Theorem~\ref{thm:merged}, and the cost results of Section~\ref{sec:cost}, but not for sufficiency); algorithms that do not change their rules when observed; known primitives $(a,\kappa)$; and stated observation contracts. Unknown primitives can only enlarge the set of candidate populations, so the lower bounds remain valid; Corollary~\ref{cor:unknown_primitives} in the supplement shows that estimating them leaves the upper-bound rate unchanged. Section~\ref{sec:equilibrium} replaces the primitive $(a,\kappa)$ by an equilibrium.

\paragraph{The observer.} The cost results are stated for one observer outside the trader groups: a supervisor, an exchange's surveillance unit or a large desk. It knows $(a,\kappa)$, the law of the observation noise and, for the $K$-group results, $K$. It can \emph{wait}, recording prices driven by natural innovations; \emph{probe}, trading a sequence $v_t$ against the groups from a reset state and recording the response; or \emph{acquire disclosure}, receiving each class's flow. Independent resets are a benchmark that no outside participant can run in a live market. We use them because they make the information content of the data exact.
