
\paragraph{In words.} A uniform cut needs only the kernel, through $S=1-\kappa/\sum_jh_j$, so public prices suffice for it. A halt of the largest group needs that group's weight, which the kernel does not pin down. The two-group populations of Theorem~\ref{thm:kcohort} (the Gauss and Gauss--Radau pair with $K=2$) have feedback budgets within $O((\Delta\eta)^3)$ of each other, but largest groups that differ at order one. So a range of ceilings exists at which halting the largest group meets the ceiling in one population and not in the other. Prices decide between them only with order $(\Delta\eta)^{-6}$ records.
