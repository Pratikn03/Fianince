\noindent The supplement contains the proofs of the results stated in the main text (Section~\ref{app:proofs}), the further equilibrium results with their proofs (Section~\ref{app:eqinv}), execution cost and capital (Section~\ref{app:capital}), the two-group case used by Theorem~\ref{prop:passive}(ii) (Section~\ref{app:twocohort}), the supervisory decision in detail, with the targeted halt for every $K$ (Section~\ref{app:decisionfull}), the proofs for saturating traders (Section~\ref{app:saturating}); then further results in four groups: identification and cost (general memory, Section~\ref{sec:general}; near-colliding rates, Section~\ref{sec:uniform}; the Cram\'er--Rao benchmark; finite-sample surveillance, Section~\ref{app:restored_finite}; real market noise, Section~\ref{sec:injection}), what prices hide in economic terms (Sections~\ref{sec:primitives}, \ref{sec:worked} and~\ref{sec:blind}), supervision, money and policy (Sections~\ref{app:restored_policy}, \ref{sec:account} and~\ref{sec:disc_dollars}), and robustness beyond the linear model (Section~\ref{sec:learners}); finally machine checks in detail (Section~\ref{app:mc}), the machine-checked declarations (Section~\ref{app:verification}) and the notation (Section~\ref{app:notation}). Proofs of results that also appear in the full working paper of 5 November 2026 are taken from it. Two things are changed in them: cross-references to material not included here, and the word for a trader group, which the full paper calls a ``co\-hort''. New in this version, with their proofs: Section~\ref{sec:saturating}, Section~\ref{app:everyK}, Propositions~\ref{prop:nearcrit} and~\ref{prop:noisystress}, and Theorems~\ref{thm:anypop}, \ref{thm:partial} and~\ref{thm:pool}. Every numerical check cited in the text is reproduced by one notebook (\nolinkurl{research_extension/theorem_checks/Paper1_Theorem_Checks_Colab.ipynb}).
