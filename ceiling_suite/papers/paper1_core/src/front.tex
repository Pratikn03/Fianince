\begin{abstract}
\noindent In a market with algorithmic feedback traders, the feedback kernel---the response of total feedback flow to past prices---is a sufficient statistic for aggregate dynamics. For any insider and causal dealer rule, populations with the same kernel generate identical prices, profits, and equilibria, and public prices identify nothing finer. Learning group composition costs the known $(\Delta\eta)^{-(4K-2)}$ mixture rate, which survives the closed loop and holds in an inventory equilibrium. Position limits introduce a second layer: a capacity measure over memory and leverage. Calm markets reveal only the linear kernel, carrying zero information about proximity to hard limits. Stress large enough to reach limits reveals the capacity measure exactly. In a pre-registered out-of-sample test on Taiwan tagged exchange flows (2019--2023), predictive gains pass the registered criterion, but pre-specified diagnostics attribute them to prices revealing past flow rather than feedback. Core statements are machine-checked in Lean~4.
\end{abstract}
