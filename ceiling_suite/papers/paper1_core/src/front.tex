\begin{abstract}
\noindent In a market with algorithmic feedback traders, the feedback kernel, the response of total feedback flow to past prices, is a sufficient statistic: for any insider and causal dealer rule, populations with the same kernel generate identical prices, profits and equilibria, and prices identify nothing finer. Learning group composition costs data of order $(\Delta\eta)^{-(4K-2)}$, in the closed loop, in equilibrium and near instability. With position limits, calm prices reveal only the kernel, and only stress reveals the rest. In a pre-registered out-of-sample test on Taiwan's tagged exchange flows (2019--2023), prices improve forecasts of foreign investors' flow as the registered criterion requires, but a pre-specified check attributes the gain to prices revealing past flow rather than to feedback. A pre-registered test of the position-limit prediction on Binance perpetual futures is inconclusive. Core statements are machine-checked in Lean~4.
\end{abstract}
