## Replication evidence

The PDFs attached to the task matched the root delivered main and combined PDFs at the base commit byte for byte. In an isolated checkout, the read-only arithmetic audit loaded the committed P1M and P1M-A1 JSON outputs. It called only `p1m_core.effects`, `kl_rate`, `pinsker_days` and `stationary` at the archived inputs and candidates. It did not execute a registered study, reconstruct the holdout, run a new optimization, or produce a revised Taiwan result.

- The primary budget vector reproduced as approximately (0.001371665, 0.000421196, −0.000126810); its sum was 0.001666050. The one-memory archived price candidate gives 1.6815883267e-12 nats per stock-day. The rate-quotient check uses the saved tagged numerator, and reproduces about 474931.39. The two calendar conversions reproduce about 2.725e9 and 9.084e6 years.
- Both original P1M panel boxes reproduced their archived endpoint effects and approximate Whittle-rate constraints at the stored candidates. The primary aggregate endpoints reproduce 0.001445391 and 0.001886583. The original P1M core hash, the A1 Part A input hash, and both A1 Part B core hashes matched their recorded values.
- During reconciliation with upstream `080ef0e`, the saved `p1m_finite_record_check.json` and its script were inspected. They compare exact Gaussian and Whittle divergence at two archived perturbations. The result supports a local diagnostic and leaves the supremum over the search set and panel dependence open.
- The Whittle-to-finite-KL step is invalid without a separate bound. For unit-innovation Gaussian AR(1) laws, use iid prices as the true law and coefficient a=0.95 under the alternative. The one-observation variance ratio is 1−a². Evaluating the Gaussian divergence gives 0.71270145, which exceeds the entropy rate a²/2=0.45125. This is an analytic counterexample evaluated with Python, unrelated to the Taiwan estimates.
- The positive Radau construction was cross-checked in Python using SciPy's Legendre and Jacobi quadrature nodes, the Radau weight formula and the exact binomial moment gap. The checked orders were K=2 through K=6. The theory proof does not depend on those finite checks.
- The original TWSE development inputs on the author's Drive, the estimates from those raw files, the tag-rate numerator and the bootstrap were not independently reproduced. The P1T registered holdout was checked only against the archived outputs and provenance logs.
