## Update (8 November 2026)

Closed:
- **Lean for the implicit-function step of Theorem 4.1(iv).** `p1_inventory_small_feedback` (`Paper1InventoryIFT.lean`)
  applies Mathlib's inverse function theorem along a one-parameter family of feedback `G = εg`. It gives existence,
  uniqueness in a fixed neighbourhood, `D'(0) = −κ₋ g(1)` and `κ'(0) = κ₋²(ā + 2Aκ₋σ_ξ²) g(1)/√Δ`. The version uniform
  over all small cohort measures remains on paper.
- **(26) A T-uniform constant for S7.1(iii).** The upper constant was already free of `T`. The lower constant is now
  free of `T` under a small-gain margin, `|a| + 2κ_max w_max θ/(θ−ρ) ≤ θ` with `r₁, r₂ ≤ ρ < θ < 1`. The constant is
  `c = (θ−ρ)⁸(1−θ)³(3+θ)/(256 κ_max⁴ (1+θ)²)`, for both weights (`p1_prop_S7_1_iii_uniform`,
  `p1_prop_S7_1_iii_two_uniform`). The margin is sufficient, not sharp. Without it, the lower constant still grows with `T`.

Both were built locally (GitHub Actions is still blocked by billing). There is no `sorry`, and only the standard axioms
are used. The count is now 1078 named declarations across 118 modules.
