# Master Claim Register (`MASTER_CLAIM_REGISTER.md`)

**Date:** 2026-10-20  
**Scope:** Authoritative claim-by-claim register across the two-paper Machine Markets program (**Paper 1**: [`ceiling_suite/paper1/`](ceiling_suite/paper1/), **Paper 2**: [`ceiling_suite/paper2/`](ceiling_suite/paper2/), and the **Paper 1 $\to$ Paper 2 Bridge**: [`ceiling_suite/bridge/BRIDGE_PROPOSITION.md`](ceiling_suite/bridge/BRIDGE_PROPOSITION.md)).

Every theoretical, formal, simulated, reduced-form empirical, and pre-registered claim in the repository is classified below by:
1. **Exact Scope & Contract**: Observation contract (`O1` controlled Gaussian reset probes, `O1'` correlated taps, `O2` exact finite window, `I1` class-tagged disclosure, `I2` passive observational prices) and Risk contract (`R` fixed position $I$, per-period shock bound $\bar u$, reset or observed initial drift $z_0=0$ vs. unreset hidden state $z_t\neq 0$).
2. **Proof / Derivation Source**: Paper proof (`p1_appendix.tex`, `p2_appendix.tex`), exact rational script, or interval-arithmetic certificate.
3. **Lean 4 Status** (synced with [`COVERAGE.md`](COVERAGE.md) and [`formal_verification_receipt.json`](research_extension/current_manuscript_lean_20261008/formal_verification_receipt.json), 443 declarations, `0 sorry`, `complete: false`):
   - **`full`**: Complete mathematical statement and domain formalized in Lean 4 + Mathlib (`CurrentProofs/`).
   - **`conditional`**: Substantive mathematical core formalized in Lean 4 + Mathlib under an explicit hypothesis for an external classical theorem or concentration step (e.g., `hSpijker`, moment-cone interiority, Rump enclosure `hencl`, Gaussian tail / Pinsker).
   - **`arithmetic`**: Algebraic identity, closed-form rearrangement, or exact rational witness verified by the Lean kernel.
4. **Numerical / Empirical Verification**: Reproducible script, Monte Carlo test (`T01`–`T15`), or pre-registered empirical evaluation (`Parts 1–18`), with negative/failed tests reported alongside passing ones.
5. **Open Obligations**: Any remaining unformalized step, scope boundary, or external specialist review requirement.
