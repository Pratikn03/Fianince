## Human decisions required

### Authorship and contribution

- Identify each human contributor and obtain consent for author order and the
  contribution statement.
- Separate conceptual work, mathematical work, code, data curation, analysis,
  writing, supervision, and review.
- Apply the target venue's authorship standard; do not infer authorship from
  commit history alone.
- Record conflicts of interest and funding.

### AI-use disclosure

- Record tools used, tasks assisted, human verification, and retained human
  responsibility for claims, code, data rights, and the final manuscript.
- Do not describe generated text or code as independent human review.

### Ethics and responsible release

- Confirm that only public, authorized data were used and that no credentials,
  private orders, or live market interventions were involved.
- Determine whether institutional, data-use, or financial-market compliance
  review is required.
- Review misuse risks: live trading, market manipulation, operational controls,
  and overinterpretation of synthetic safety results.
- Require the manuscript to retain failed cases and claim limitations.

### Data and publication rights

- Verify the Binance archive terms for intended redistribution, retention, and
  derived-data release.
- Decide whether raw data remain external while manifests and summaries are
  released.
- Complete venue-specific conflict, disclosure, artifact, and data statements.
