# Integration and source provenance

The integrated manuscript is based on the original 24-page framework, not a standalone replacement. Sections 1-7 preserve its framing, related literature, augmented Jacobian, local/transient/switching distinctions, tractable network boundary and general measurement discussion. The original intervention discussion, numerical algebra program and prospective flagship validation program remain later in the paper.

Section 8 integrates the 15-page Hidden Slow Learners companion: positive heterogeneous-memory boundary, exact matched construction, rational witness, summary-tail interval, original execution counts and assumptions.

Section 9 integrates the 13-page Full-Horizon Identification Audit: stronger conventional comparators, adverse original-estimator comparison, four wrong high-noise confident decisions, confidence-event analysis and the fixed-order kernel-law observation limit. Its true title is Hidden Slow Learners Revisited: Full-Horizon Positive Identification and Limits of Stability Measurement.

Sections 10-13 are newly executed for this integration: public signed-order/price observations; regression covariance and finite-family uncertainty; response-only positive recurrence bounds; account-consistent market mechanism; primary study; exploratory budgets; adverse controls; separate price-law lower bound; and verification.

No exact statement in either original companion has been silently promoted into a claim about noisy public prices. The earlier iid kernel observation law and the new correlated price-response observation law are kept distinct. The new manuscript revises the original 'all market experiments unexecuted' disclosure to distinguish the completed dealer measurement pilot from unexecuted trained-agent and real-institution studies.

The three source PDFs/TeX files are preserved unchanged, alongside selected old machine-readable results. Lightweight copies of old source/test directories support rechecking the 29 and 23 legacy tests. Complete old experiment tables remain in their separate prior releases; source archive hashes are recorded. The new package does not claim to bundle those complete historical data releases.

The original concept note proposed ambitions, not observed evidence. Its source-to-manuscript revisions are retained in Appendix C. No author identity or affiliation has been invented.
