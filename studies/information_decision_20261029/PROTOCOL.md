# Frozen-in-source exploratory protocol

Creation date: 29 October 2026. A saved configuration and commit are provenance,
not an independent preregistration. No acceptance, grade, or foundational status
is inferred. All regimes, budgets, N values, policies and repetitions must remain
in the export, including failures. Do not select a seed based on its results.

Primary exploratory comparisons: paid disclosure versus aggregate certificate;
aggregate certificate versus volatility-plus-persistence and robust empirical
log utility. Report net log wealth, cost, initial-wealth loss, peak drawdown,
breach frequency and average inventory together. No single metric proves dominance.
Information comparisons hold return-training data and objective fixed.

Full profile: 20 independent repetitions for each of seven regimes, N=8,32,128
reset readings, loss budgets .5,1,2,4, 768 training, 384 validation, 512 test
episodes. Common external tapes permit paired policy comparisons. Each episode
is a separate synthetic 60-minute account. N counts additional noisy impulse
experiments, NOT the total information in training and validation paths.
All budget points form the published frontier; no test-outcome selection.

The bootstrap resamples independent repetition-level paired means. It does not
pretend correlated budgets, N values, policies or metrics are independent.
Intervals are exploratory, pointwise and NOT corrected for the full search family.
Smoke profile is a software check only, with two repetitions, N=16 and two budgets.

Valid regimes: no_feedback, moderate_feedback, near_boundary. Invalid-contract
diagnostics: hidden_slow_memory, correlated_readings, biased_readings,
behavior_change. No empirical certification claim is allowed in invalid regimes.

Optional market CSV is a distinct observational forecast exercise. One-minute
or five-minute regular bars; six-hour lookback; next-hour absolute excursion;
hourly nonoverlapping forecasts; chronological 60/20/20 splits with a target-
interval purge; transformations fitted only on training; ridge tuned on validation.
No labels from test tune the rule. Existing sample reuse must be documented by
the author. Executed flow is not Cont-style order-flow imbalance; a genuine OFI
benchmark needs appropriate quote updates and is marked unavailable here.
The optional exercise never grants a structural risk certificate or measures P&L.

Preconditions for a later confirmatory study: settle the institution and data
channel, independently review central proofs and literature comparisons, choose
one economic endpoint and meaningful effect, fix sample/exclusions/tuning and
record the protocol at an independent timestamp BEFORE new test data are viewed.

The blank paper stays empty. Tables/plots go to the results directory only.
After results are returned, human review decides which claims can populate it.
