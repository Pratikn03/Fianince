# Contribution boundary — October 8, 2026

This is a provisional contribution statement, not a priority or novelty certificate.

The defensible candidate contribution is a contract-specific analysis of what a reset price-response experiment, richer disclosure and an authenticated financial input/output mapping can establish, accompanied by a reconstruction of measurement-sensitive market diagnostics. The paper must demonstrate that this combination supplies an advance beyond the existing tools, rather than claim those tools as inventions.
