Chapter 1
Purpose and Protocol
This dossier records the canonical simulated 15-reviewer audit for the live ORIUS defense package.
The package is grounded in repo truth: the active three-domain lane (Battery + AV + Healthcare),
the machine-generated active theorem audit, the canonical build pathmake review-compile, and
the generated publication artifacts underreports/publication/.
ORIUS is defended as one bounded stack:
•Phenomenon: the Observation–Action Safety Gap (OASG),
•Mechanism: reliability-aware runtime safety and calibration under degraded observation,
•Evidence lane: Battery as witness row, AV and Healthcare as bounded defended peer rows.
The single flagship novelty sentence used across the defense package is:
ORIUS provides a reliability-aware runtime safety layer for physical AI under degraded
observation, enforcing certificate-backed action release through uncertainty coverage, re-
pair, and fallback.
Novelty boundary
The novelty claim is architectural and runtime-semantic: ORIUS does not claim a new conformal
method, a new robust-optimization primitive, a new universal controller, or a new conditional-
coverage theorem.
Reviewer-facing separation is explicit:
•conformal methods contribute uncertainty sets, but not repaired actuation or release certifi-
cation;
•runtime monitors and anomaly detectors contribute alarms or vetoes, but not tightened gov-
erned release;
•simplex and runtime-assurance schemes contribute supervisory override and fallback, but not
reliability-conditioned tightening under one shared release contract; and
•safety filters and robust MPC contribute plant-local safe action geometry, but not a reusable
degraded-observation release grammar across domains.

Chapter 3
Final Disposition Summary
The canonical defense package doesnotclaim that every theorem-like row in the repository is
equally rigorous. It claims something narrower and more defensible:
1. the live defended program is the promoted Battery + AV + Healthcare lane,
2. the registry-canonical theorem core is explicitly classified into flagship, supporting, and draft
surfaces,
3. proof rigor is tracked by the active theorem audit rather than by a traceability gate pretending
to be a proof checker, and
4. any row that still lacks aligned code, tests, proofs, and bounded scope wording is demoted
rather than rhetorically preserved.
That is the repo-truthful ORIUS defense posture after hardening: a smaller, auditable, reviewer-
safe claim surface intended to survive hostile reading.
