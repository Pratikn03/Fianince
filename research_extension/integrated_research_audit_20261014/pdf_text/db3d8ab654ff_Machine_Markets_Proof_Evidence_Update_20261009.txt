1 Precise contribution
The proposed increment is the joint specification of the observer, execution resource and financing
account. Two known latent cohort configurations produce scalar price observations. For deter-
ministic probes, a lifetime absolute-quantity budget has an exact finite-horizon signal optimum.
Combining the resolution calculation with a self-financing account gives a distinct capital order.
Exact marked-equity disclosure supplies a constructive counterexample to a universal capital bar-
rier.
The relevant observer is an outside analyst receiving noisy aggregate price reports and probe quan-
tities without the financing trader’s exact execution/account records. An account owner with exact
marks has a different experiment. Quotation residuals mix dynamics, specification error and other
noise; they do not identify the theorem’s independent measurement-noise variance.
