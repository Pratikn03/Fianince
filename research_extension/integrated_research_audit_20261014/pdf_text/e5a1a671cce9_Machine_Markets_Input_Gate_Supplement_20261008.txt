3 Regulatory Context and the Remaining Operational Gate
SEC Rule 15c3-5 already requires pre-trade credit/capital and erroneous-order controls; it is in-
accurate to frame market-access protection as exclusively or inherently post-trade. See the SEC
adopting release and staff guidance: adopting release, staff FAQ. The FAQ is staff guidance rather
than the rule itself. The proposed mathematical quantity bound may inform a risk-control design
under its assumptions; it does not establish regulatory compliance, welfare superiority or execution
feasibility.
Operational use requires authenticated positions, marks, currencies, executions, fees, funding,
margins and cancellations; justified input, state, model-error and noise bounds; and carried expo-
sure/capital accounting. Empirical model rejections and reserve calibration remain unresolved.
