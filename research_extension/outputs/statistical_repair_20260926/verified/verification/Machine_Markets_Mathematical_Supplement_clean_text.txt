A real matrix is Metzler when every off-diagonal entry is nonnegative. It is Hurwitz when all
eigenvalues have negative real part. The following established criterion is included with a proof
so the application is self-contained [2].

S10      What coverage guarantees, and why abstention cannot dis-

Theorem S10.1 (Directionally valid classifications). Suppose an interval [LS , US ] covers the
true S with probability at least 1 − α. Issue STABLE if US < 1, UNSTABLE if LS > 1, and
ABSTAIN otherwise; an empty or unverified set is a separate non-certificate status. Then the
unconditional probability of a wrong strict label is at most α. If the probability of issuing a strict
label is c > 0, the implied conditional bound is at most min{1, α/c}, not automatically α.

Proof. On the coverage event, US < 1 implies S < 1, and LS > 1 implies S > 1. Thus any wrong
strict label is contained in the noncoverage event. Divide its probability by c for the conditional
statement. When c = 0, conditional error is undefined. At S = 1 either strict label is wrong.

Theorem S10.2 (Two-world abstention bound). Let P0 , P1 be observation laws for opposite
strict stability classes. Under equal class weights, let c be the probability of issuing either strict
label and e the unconditional wrong-label probability. Then

                                   c ≤ min{1, 2e + TV(P0 , P1 )}.

If the two laws are identical, c = 2e. For equal-covariance Gaussian laws with Mahalanobis mean
distance d, the bound is
                                  c ≤ min{1, 2e + 2Φ(d/2) − 1}.

                           c = 12 {P0 (A0 ) + P0 (A1 ) + P1 (A0 ) + P1 (A1 )},
                           e = 21 {P0 (A1 ) + P1 (A0 )}.

Subtracting gives

                 c − 2e = 21 {P0 (A0 ) − P1 (A0 ) + P1 (A1 ) − P0 (A1 )} ≤ TV(P0 , P1 ).

         ments

S11.1     A precise solvency non-transfer example

Proposition S11.1 (No solvency guarantee from a stable signal alone). Fix any scalar signal
persistence r ∈ [0, 1) and any π ∈ [0, 1]. There is a finite-price, initially solvent account and an
external-shock law with signal transition coefficient r for which insolvency at a specified horizon
has probability π.

Proof. Take price Pt = P0 + xt with xt+1 = rxt + εt+1 , initially x0 = 0. Let a fixed account hold
I > 0 shares and have equity E0 > 0; choose P0 > 2E0 /I and cash C = E0 − IP0 . The initial
equity is C + IP0 = E0 . Assume funding permits this initial position; the proposition specifies
no particular leverage or credit rule. Set all innovations before the designated time to zero. At
that time let the innovation be −2E0 /I with probability π and zero otherwise. On the shock
event the price remains positive but the equity becomes E0 + I(−2E0 /I) = −E0 . Otherwise it
remains E0 . The signal coefficient is still r < 1, while insolvency probability is exactly π.

This is a scope counterexample, not a new liquidity-spiral theory. Its purpose is to show that
shock distributions, exposures, collateral, and execution constraints cannot be inferred from a
stable eigenvalue. The manuscript’s funded simulator must still measure its own predeclared
scenario probabilities and mechanism contrasts.


S11.2     Why a theoretical solution does not finish the empirical study

The response ellipsoid proves coverage only under (S14). It does not prove that one historical
market stream supplies iid reset episodes. The continuous-time theorem does not prove that
actual agents obey the model. The group theorem does not show which real accounts are adaptive.
A new interval construction does not prove an improvement over the original method until both
are evaluated with matched inputs, coverage targets, and numerical safeguards.
Similarly, computing features on Period A and fitting a regression using Period B outcomes does
not by itself measure forecasting performance of a rule frozen before those B outcomes were
seen. A later untouched date set remains necessary for that particular predictive claim. Missing
historical spread or depth observations must be removed from the corresponding primary analysis
or explicitly restricted to a retrospective diagnostic.
The correct remaining empirical work is to report interval coverage, widths, decision exposure,
conditional and unconditional errors, model conflicts, runtime, and actual forward predictive
loss under a frozen protocol. A null result, noncomparability finding, or frequent abstention is
admissible. No proof here guarantees a grade, acceptance, a target p-value, or a fixed percentage
of project completion.

1. Freeze the response dimension, probe design, observation contract, rate floor, and error level

2. Compute the OLS response, residual covariance, residual degrees of freedom, and exact F

3. Form the ellipsoid, its nonnegative coordinate enclosure, and the finite mass cap. Record

4. Build continuous rate-bin envelopes and the lifted conic recurrence program. Do not replace
   continuous support with a bare grid without a declared approximation.
5. Solve both endpoint problems; retain primal, dual, residual, and gap information. Return
   certificates only from outward-valid endpoint bounds.
6. Optionally refine response subboxes and rate bins while retaining valid bounds for every
   unresolved region.
7. Score the new and retained methods on identical I0 panels. Keep I1 comparisons separate or
   explicitly label their stronger information.
8. Generate every table and claim from saved result rows. Preserve unsuccessful solves and
   undefined conditional-error denominators.

S14.2    Environment and economic accounting

The experimental environment should contain an explicit central exchange matching engine,
finite cash and inventory balances, and price formation via double-auction books. Every fill
transfers inventory and cash between counterparties, with trading fees and margin accounted
for. In a leveraged extension, maintenance margin calculations and forced liquidations must be
audited. ABIDES is a candidate infrastructure [1].


S14.3    Agent families and paired baselines

A complete design compares: (i) adaptive expectation chartists, (ii) value-learning agents (e.g.,
DQN / PPO), (iii) expressive deep actor-critic policies, and (iv) inference-only language-model
agents. Each adaptive policy requires a paired baseline with the identical checkpoint and
architecture but online adaptation frozen.


S14.4    Corrected tabular benchmark and recorded support

The main paper’s corrective inventory-only experiment replaces the interpretation of the earlier
tabular transfer study. Its ten policies use 100,000 transitions each, learning rate 0.05, discount
factor 0.98, and multiplicative exploration decay 0.99995 with floor 0.02. Training seeds are
926101–926110; checkpoint validation uses 926201–926205 and evaluation uses 926301–926310.
Validation cannot mutate training states or randomness. Active and frozen evaluation arms
restart from matched complete checkpoints for every tape.
The revised checkpoints contain state–action update counts, hyperparameters and the random
generator state. Nonzero Q entries alone are not visit counts: a visited action can retain a
zero estimate. Missing counts in legacy checkpoints are labeled unavailable, never retroactively
reconstructed as measured visits. The response screen holds other state coordinates fixed and
averages over tied greedy actions. Unsupported queries cause abstention; a constant response
has undefined R2 , not evidence of a class violation.
The complete per-policy counts, training histories, and selected checkpoints are in research_
extension/outputs/evidence_repair_20260926/learned_agents_v2/. The main paper’s
generated table reports the corrected outcomes. These results are post-review diagnostics,
not preregistered confirmation, and are not pooled with the legacy study.

S16      Legal and regulatory governance of active probing

S16.1    Market abuse regulations and manipulative intent

Under market manipulation frameworks such as SEC Rule 10b-5 in the United States and
Article 12 of the EU Market Abuse Regulation (MAR), submitting orders or executing transactions
that give false or misleading signals regarding the supply of, demand for, or price of financial
instruments is strictly prohibited. Executing orders without genuine investment intent to probe
counterparty algorithms can trigger regulatory scrutiny under manipulative and deceptive practice
standards.


S16.2    Operational boundaries for supervisory authorities

Consequently, active probing is established in this research strictly as a theoretical identification
benchmark, not an operational trading recommendation. If an exchange operator or macropru-
dential authority evaluates active system identification, any physical execution protocol requires
explicit legal, regulatory, and supervisory authorization. Standard market-wide mechanisms,
such as the national Limit-Up/Limit-Down (LULD) Plan, impose non-discriminatory price bands
and trading pauses precisely because live causal attribution across heterogeneous participants
remains an open institutional challenge.

"""Deterministic␣algebra␣checks␣for␣Machine␣Markets.
Requires␣Python␣3␣and␣NumPy.␣Run:␣python␣verify_math.py
"""
import json
import numpy as np

def spectral_radius(matrix):
    matrix = np.asarray(matrix, dtype=float)
    if matrix.ndim != 2 or matrix.shape[0] != matrix.shape[1]:
        raise ValueError("Expected␣a␣square␣matrix")
    if not np.isfinite(matrix).all():
        raise ValueError("Nonfinite␣matrix␣entry")
    return float(np.max(np.abs(np.linalg.eigvals(matrix))))

def scalar_matrix(a, g, eta, kappa=1.0):
    if not (0 <= a < 1 and 0 < eta <= 1 and g >= 0 and kappa >= 0):
        raise ValueError("Parameters␣violate␣proposition␣assumptions")
    return np.array([[a, g], [eta * kappa, 1 - eta]])

def rho_formula(a, g, eta, kappa=1.0):
    return float((a + 1 - eta + np.sqrt((a - 1 + eta)**2 + 4 * g * eta * kappa)) / 2)

def main():
    rows = []
    for label, g, eta in [
        ("stable␣feedback", 0.30, 0.20),
        ("boundary", 0.60, 0.20),
        ("unstable␣feedback", 0.90, 0.20),
        ("same␣unstable␣gain,␣slower␣learning", 0.90, 0.05),
        ("same␣unstable␣gain,␣faster␣learning", 0.90, 0.80),
        ("baseline␣before␣action␣gate", 0.45, 0.20),
        ("gate,␣depth␣unchanged", 0.225, 0.20),
        ("gate,␣depth␣reduced␣to␣one␣quarter", 0.90, 0.20),
    ]:
        radius = spectral_radius(scalar_matrix(0.4, g, eta))
        assert abs(radius - rho_formula(0.4, g, eta)) < 1e-12
        rows.append(dict(case=label, a=0.4, g=g, eta=eta, kappa=1.0, radius=radius))

   # Deterministic Cartesian grid
   scalar_checks = 0
   max_error = 0.0
   for a in [0.1, 0.3, 0.5, 0.7, 0.9]:
       for eta in [0.05, 0.2, 0.5, 0.8, 1.0]:
            for g in [0.07, 0.17, 0.37, 0.67, 1.07]:
                for kappa in [0.15, 0.45, 0.85, 1.25, 1.75]:
                    radius = spectral_radius(scalar_matrix(a, g, eta, kappa))
                    max_error = max(max_error, abs(radius - rho_formula(a, g, eta,
   kappa)))
                    assert abs(g * kappa - (1 - a)) > 1e-8
                    assert (radius < 1) == (g * kappa < 1 - a)
                    scalar_checks += 1

    # Independent network formula checks
    rng = np.random.default_rng(20260918)
    network_checks = 0
    max_network_error = 0.0
    for _ in range(250):
        d, n = 3, 4
        B = rng.uniform(0, 1, (d, n)) / n
        C = rng.uniform(0, 1, (n, d)) / d
        a = float(rng.uniform(0.05, 0.95))
        eta = float(rng.uniform(0.05, 1.0))
        g = float(rng.uniform(0.01, 4.0))
        J = np.block([[a*np.eye(d), g*B], [eta*C, (1-eta)*np.eye(n)]])
        nu = spectral_radius(B @ C)
        radius = spectral_radius(J)
        pred = rho_formula(a, g, eta, nu)

        assert (radius < 1) == (g*nu < 1-a)

    J2 = J1.T
    switch_radius = spectral_radius(J2 @ J1)
    assert spectral_radius(J1) == 0.5 and switch_radius > 1

S17.1    Correction of the legacy response and reward diagnostics

The archived multiscale audit queried price and return jointly and used one random greedy tie
draw per perturbation. It called nonzero Q entries support and assigned R2 = 0 to a constant
response. Those choices do not establish that a trained policy violates the positive-memory
model. In particular, an identically zero local response is compatible with zero local gain. The
corrected support-first audit in the main paper withholds a structural conclusion for all ten
policies.
The historical reward-penalty sweep is retained in the archive but not treated as evidence from
the corrected economic environment. It used the earlier capacity/accounting implementation and
response screen. No externally timestamped preregistration is established here, so its preregistered
designation is withdrawn. A future reward intervention must use corrected accounting and
independently specified evaluation.
The old per-policy random comparator used test-set trading frequencies and unit-sized orders.
Matching frequency is not matching realized turnover, and conditioning a comparator on test
outcomes is post-hoc. The corrective pipeline chooses frequency from validation only and reports
the realized volume difference explicitly. It does not claim to isolate liquidity provision or the
causal role of inventory penalties.
