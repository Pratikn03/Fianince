# Central contribution and nearest-work comparison

The current candidate centers on a precise resource distinction for a controlled memory-identification experiment. It does not claim first use of Prony methods, non-normal financial dynamics, Gaussian testing or Kreiss bounds.

## Proven result now included

For the known single-cohort versus symmetric split-cohort pair, with positive feedback, zero initial state, no process noise and independent Gaussian price-observation noise:

- The exact best error under a deterministic lifetime quantity allowance V is Phi(-V times the finite impulse-response difference norm / (2 sigma)). A first pulse and the full Gaussian likelihood-ratio test attain it.
- Required total executed quantity has matching necessary/sufficient order delta^-2. Information from waiting saturates at fixed lifetime quantity.
- With average power P instead, the required horizon has matching order delta^-4. A concentrated pulse attains that order; its growing size is an essential part of the protocol.
- The physical positive-cohort price kernel is a positive mixture of decaying exponentials. Price-only initial-state gain in the declared coordinates is one; full-state non-normality alone does not establish monetary price amplification.
- Refreshed average-book fills give actual probe-trader execution cost one-half v-transpose T_h v. Solvency after temporary impact dissipates bounds probe energy, giving necessary capital order delta^-4 for any finite deterministic signed probe. A positive pulse financed by a margin loan attains that order. This result does not impose a lifetime quantity cap.
- A separate short counterparty account gives an exact robust nonnegative-quantity allowance from cash, impact, fees and margin. Its capital order inside that robust allowance is also delta^-4, but it is not the same funding account or an optimal-constant trader result.
- Exact marked-equity disclosure distinguishes the known pair with zero error for any admissible positive pulse. Independent naturally excited reset blocks also permit passive distinction with zero controlled quantity. These counterexamples make the observation/innovation contract essential.

Full proofs are in the standalone `Machine_Markets_Central_Result_Review_20261008.tex` and its four source bodies under `research_extension/gap_closure_20261006/publication_completion/`. The 87-check complete suite includes exact transfer/tap algebra, optimal quantity pulse, actual cash/inventory, positive response geometry, quadratic-cost energy, two-account conservation and an exact fourth-moment check of the passive counterexample. These checks supplement the written proofs rather than establish novelty or external validation.

The lower capital order requires eventual solvency after temporary impact dissipates, positive true prices, known common primitives, no process noise, finite deterministic signed probes and a statistical observer restricted to noisy price states and known quantities. Attainability assumes a margin loan, no holding interest/fundamental risk and sufficient linear-model validity for growing probes. It does not solve unknown mixtures, feedback-adaptive optimality, stochastic-market calibration or a universal account-owner capital barrier.

## Proposed contribution and falsifiable novelty test

The exact claim proposed for expert assessment is: **in the declared positive-cohort, noiseless-state financial experiment, known symmetric memory coalescence induces sharp lifetime-quantity and funded deterministic-probe capital orders, and those limits change under richer financial disclosure or natural excitation.** The financial capital result applies to all finite deterministic signed probes that remain solvent at the limiting fundamental mark, rather than only a declared quantity ball.

This is more precise than the earlier lower-bound-only or finance-non-normality claim. It combines established tools; an expert may judge the combination a limited corollary rather than an important journal advance. The targeted primary comparison has not identified an identical stated theorem, but it is not an exhaustive priority proof. To close this gate, a specialist must either identify an equivalent prior result and force a further advance, or substantiate why the capital/account/observation distinction is new and sufficiently important. Unknown-mixture or adaptive results may be useful future advances, but are not quietly claimed as solved here.

No 9+ rating or publication-ready certification follows from these proofs, comparisons or local tests.
