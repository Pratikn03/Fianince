### Paper 1 Claim Reconciliation

| # | Manuscript Claim Location | Raw Claim in Baseline Manuscript | Exact Verified Status | Required Manuscript Reconciliation & Qualification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1-1** | Abstract & Intro | "Telling a single cohort from a split pair needs executed volume $V = \Theta(\delta^{-2})$ and capital $B = \Theta(\delta^{-4})$." | `PROVED` | **Clarify**: $V = \Theta(\delta^{-2})$ requires concentrating volume in an unsplit pulse of size $\Theta(\delta^{-2})$. When individual trade size is capped at $v_{\max}$, total executed volume across cycles scales as $\Theta(v_{\max}^{-1}\delta^{-4})$. Committed margin capital on a single unhedged pulse is $\Theta(\delta^{-4})$, while cumulative irrevocable round-trip execution dissipation across repeated flat cycles is $\Omega(\delta^{-4})$ even when margin capital is reused across cycles. |
| **P1-2** | Section 7.2 | "For $K$ cohorts, the minimax rate is $N = \Theta((\Delta\eta)^{-(4K-2)})$." | `CLAIM_REQUIRES_REVISION` | **Revise**: The lower bound $N = \Omega(\sigma^2(\Delta\eta)^{-(4K-2)})$ is proved for the Gauss vs. Gauss–Radau construction (`thm:kcohort`). For the matching upper bound (`prop:esprit`), the inverse Jacobian rows scale as $(\Delta\eta)^{-(2K-2)}$ for memory rates and $(\Delta\eta)^{-(2K-1)}$ for weights. The minimax rate holds under the scale-matched metric $\max_i(|\hat r_i - r_i|, \frac{\Delta\eta}{W}|\hat w_i - w_i|)$, and Wasserstein-1 estimation achieves rate $(\Delta\eta)^{-(4K-4)}$ via Lipschitz cancellation. Explicitly distinguish this from estimating an unknown number of cohorts $K$. |
| **P1-3** | Section 7.3 | "For general completely monotone memory, $\varepsilon_N = \Theta(1/\log N)$." | `CONDITIONALLY_PROVED` | **Qualify**: State explicitly that the upper and lower bounds hold for any **fixed** memory bound $\rho < 1$, with constants depending explicitly on $(1-\rho)^{-1}$ and $\log B(\rho)$. As $\rho \to 1$, the upper and lower bounds diverge because the moment-matched pairs separate more slowly. |
| **P1-4** | Section 8.1 & Table 8 | "Closed-loop trained online learners settle to local exponential dynamics obeying merged-measure equivalence and the $\delta^{-4}$ law." | `PROVED` (after bugfix) | **Disclose & Update**: Disclose that baseline `recompute_p1_learners.py` bypassed the `b_info` checkpoint dict and evaluated surrogate unit gains, and that `q_profit_trained` was a scalar profit-gradient learner. Report the hardened results from `validate_trained_learners_hardened.py` across all four genuinely trained learners (including the $21\times 21$ tabular Bellman Q-learner) across Regimes 1a, 1b, 2, and 3, noting that the $\delta^{-4}$ barrier applies when competing hypotheses are moment-matched in aggregate sensitivity. |
| **P1-5** | Section 8.3 & Table 18 | "Exchange trader-class tags improve 30-minute positioning forecasts by $+50.7\text{ pt}$ OOS $R^2$." | `PROVED` (as statistical fit) | **Qualify**: Disclose that `pf_cols` omitted current positioning levels whereas `tag_cols` included them; $99.1\%$–$100.5\%$ of the $+50.7\text{ pt}$ gain is captured by a simple univariate AR(1) benchmark ($y_{t+6} \approx \rho^6 y_t$). ### Paper 2 Claim Reconciliation

| # | Manuscript Claim Location | Raw Claim in Baseline Manuscript | Exact Verified Status | Required Manuscript Reconciliation & Qualification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P2-1** | Abstract & Intro | "Pre-trade risk certification requires only the scalar summed gain $S = \kappa/(1-a-\kappa\Phi)$, which is independent of cohort memory rates." | `PROVED` | **Boundary Condition**: Explicitly emphasize that $\|h\|_1 = S$ holds if and only if closed-loop impulse responses are nonnegative ($h_t \ge 0$, which holds under positive momentum $q_i \ge 0$ and mild inventory damping by `prop:inventory_S`). When contrarian trading ($q_i < 0$) or strong inventory payback causes $h_t$ to change sign, $\|h\|_1 > |S|$ (Counterexample C1), and the guard must evaluate the full $\ell_1$ norm. |
| **P2-2** | Section 5.1 & 6.1 | "Pre-trade guard admission $\alpha^* = c_I / (\kappa\Phi^+)$ guarantees forward solvency." | `PROVED` (under zero initial state) | **Scope Restriction**: Emphasize that the scalar formula $\alpha^*$ assumes an explicitly reset initial market state ($z_0 = 0$). When hidden cohort memory is non-zero ($z_0 \neq 0$), unforced drift consumes margin buffer according to the exact envelope derived in Proposition `prop:driftguard`, requiring an explicit admission haircut $\alpha_{\mathrm{drift}}^* < \alpha^*$. |
| **P2-3** | Section 5.3 & Thm 5.3 | "From a finite window of impulse responses, the compatible sensitivity range is $[\Phi_-, \Phi_+]$." | `PROVED` | **Essential Boundary Condition**: Highlight Theorem `thm:window`(iii): if memory rates are permitted to approach $1$ ($\bar r = 1$), $\Phi_+ = \infty$ for any finite window $n$, proving that certifying stability or risk from price data is impossible without a prior bound $\bar r < 1$ on memory persistence. |
| **P2-4** | Section 6.2 & Thm 6.1 | "The pre-trade guard is maximal." | `PROVED` | **Clarify**: Maximality is proved specifically over the declared admissible class of constant scaling actions $\alpha \in [0, 1]$ on unhedged client orders subject to deterministic $\ell_\infty$ disturbance bounds; it is not claimed as an unconditional property of all dynamic non-linear control policies. |
| **P2-5** | Section 10.2 & Table 24 | "The combined guard reduces forced liquidation rates by $-7.41\text{ pp}$ and expands capacity." | `QUALIFIED` | **Reconcile**: Disclose that the baseline throttle reduced average notional exposure by $11.4\%$ (from $\$829\text{k}$ to $\$735\text{k}$), which accounts for much of the baseline breach reduction. Under matched training notional exposure (Benchmark 4B-ii), breach rate reduction is indistinguishable from zero ($\Delta = +0.25\text{ pp}$, $95\%\text{ CI}: [-5.64, +5.16]$). However, the guard delivers substantial risk-adjusted value in **reducing maximum drawdown catastrophic loss by $28.5\%$** (from $\$623\text{k}$ to $\$445\text{k}$) and **expanding admitted capacity by $+7.9\%$ at matched training risk budget ($\mathrm{ES}_{95}$)**. Separate stylized 450 bp two-sided excursions from realistic directional long/short losses ($14.3\%$ long, $18.3\%$ short). |
| **P2-6** | Section 10.3 & Table 25 | "The multi-minute persistence mechanism mediates $98.6\%$ of the excursion forecasting gain." | `FAILED PRIMARY PRESERVED` | **Preserve & Reconcile**: Report that the pre-registered Primary 2 test failed because the 5-minute single-bar jump placebo mediated $78.6\% \ge 50\%$. Explain the mathematical reason: $\mathrm{MAE}_{60}$, $M_{\mathrm{ckpt}}$, and $M_{\mathrm{l1}}$ are contemporaneous summary norms of the same 60-minute trajectory $p_f$, showing $r > 0.92$ correlation even under a pure Gaussian random-walk null. Add the hardened, non-overlapping sequential persistence results showing that $\hat C_3$ forecasts initial 15-minute price displacement ($\Delta R^2 = +3.32\text{ pt}$), which attenuates subsequent excursion predictability by $65.1\%$ (level) and $40.3\%$ (incremental travel). |
