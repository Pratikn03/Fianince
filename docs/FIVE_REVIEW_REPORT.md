# Five-review disposition

The five internal review passes are stored in `docs/reviews/`:

1. reproducibility and evidence integrity;
2. statistical design and denominators;
3. market microstructure and historical L2 calibration;
4. AI comparison, causality, and operational risk;
5. novelty, authorship, ethics, and publication readiness.

## Overall disposition

The branch is suitable for a limited, internally reproducible research
checkpoint. The historical public corpus materially strengthens the transfer
boundary, but it is a depth-band/trade-tape calibration rather than a complete
queue-level L2 replay. The trained-policy experiment is a scoped synthetic
null, and the intervention result is a local synthetic mechanism. Human and
venue gates are prepared, not completed.

## Non-negotiable claim boundaries

- No full L2, queue-priority, fill-probability, or spread claim is promoted.
- No real-market safety guarantee or operational intervention claim is
  promoted.
- No AI-specific causal effect is promoted.
- No independent replication, novelty determination, authorship decision,
  ethics approval, data-rights clearance, or venue acceptance is fabricated.
