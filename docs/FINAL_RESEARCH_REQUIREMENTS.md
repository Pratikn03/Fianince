# What a completed flagship contribution still needs

This is a prospective acceptance specification, not a claim that these gates have been passed.
It preserves the distinction already made in the v0.5 manuscript. Document length, additional
simulation rows, or successful unit tests do not close these gaps.

## Gate 1 — Scientific priority and independent proof review
**Required output:** a nearest-predecessor comparison for each claimed result, with a human
expert's proof report, corrected assumptions and explicit contribution statements.
**Current status:** targeted literature notes and internal proofs are supplied. Positive-systems
bounds, regression intervals, binomial intervals, moment methods and data informativity are
acknowledged predecessors. Global priority and external proof approval are unestablished.
**Claim limit:** do not call the spectral radius, geometric decay or conventional confidence tools
new theories. Separate an application-specific construction from a new general theorem.

## Gate 2 — Authenticated model boundary
**Required output:** independently checked source/runtime inventory of all memory, execution,
funding, inventory and update channels, including resets and changes during observation.
**Current status:** controlled source/configuration contracts are implemented; no institutional
runtime has been authenticated. A checksum proves agreement with a file, not truth or completeness.
**Acceptance test:** omitted/changed channels must be detected or trigger withheld conclusions.
The 80/80 failure control remains evidence of the risk until such a mechanism is demonstrated.

## Gate 3 — Realistic, costed observations
**Required output:** protocol with a declared budget in independent episodes, elapsed time,
filled volume, price impact, inventory/unwind cost, information access and computational expense.
Derive uncertainty for noisy regressors, dependent records, unknown initial states and partial fills
rather than reusing a theorem whose assumptions fail.
**Current status:** synthetic protocols with strong disclosure, exact prices and independent resets.
**Acceptance test:** compare equally informed estimators at matched economic costs and report
wrong-label frequencies, unresolved fractions and model conflicts, including near-boundary cases.

## Gate 4 — Second economically calibrated implementation
**Required output:** independently written market implementation tied to a documented empirical
or controlled-laboratory setting, with explicit credit, leverage, inventory, liquidity and execution.
**Current status:** one uncalibrated synthetic transaction model with binding constraints.
**Acceptance test:** reproduce the mechanism without relying on the original simulator's internals;
verify conservation, fills, liquidation, dealer stress and sensitivity to calibration.

## Gate 5 — AI-specific mechanism and transfer
**Required output:** matched adaptive non-AI, frozen-policy and trained-AI baselines under the same
information, objectives, capital, risk limits and execution conditions.
**Current status:** no trained neural-agent population or demonstrated uniquely AI-caused effect.
**Acceptance test:** isolate the causal contribution of adaptation/architecture from ordinary
funding feedback, shared signals and microstructure; report null and unfavorable comparisons.
A broad critical surface or global machine-autonomy threshold has not been established.

## Gate 6 — Operational validity and interventions
**Required output:** a separate operational-loss target with out-of-sample evaluation, calibration,
false alarms, missed events, uncertainty and transfer across realistic regimes.
**Current status:** synthetic event-probability intervals, with easy primary probability extremes.
**Acceptance test:** evaluate difficult intermediate risks and show whether an intervention reduces
loss rather than merely suppressing local feedback or shifting harm to another account. Do not
extrapolate a linear local certificate through binding nonlinear constraints.

## Gate 7 — Outside replication
**Required output:** a named external researcher's dated report, independently written code,
source/input hashes, reproduction logs, discrepancies, and a revised manuscript response.
**Current status:** pending. Shipped internal verifiers and fresh-directory reruns are not outside
replication. No external name, institution or approval is fabricated.
**Starting point:** `studies/audited_constraints/replication/EXTERNAL_REPLICATION.md` and the
challenge/reference files; a reviewer should avoid reading the answers while implementing.

## Gate 8 — Publication and responsible release
**Required output:** confirmed human authorship/contributions, appropriate AI-use disclosure,
rights/data permissions, final venue formatting and independent assessment of scope and claims.
**Current status:** research-development manuscripts and artifacts only; no acceptance, DOI,
endorsement or deployment assurance. Investigational experiments belong in controlled settings,
not unauthorized live-market perturbations.

## The final scientific paper should contain
A sharply stated economic question; nearest-predecessor comparison; exact information contract;
proofs and scope limits; operational measurement protocol; fair conventional baselines; calibrated
mechanism tests; adverse and failed cases; out-of-sample or cross-setting validation; uncertainty
and cost analysis; an outside-replication report; data/code availability and rights; and conclusions
whose scope matches those outputs. Existing sections satisfy parts of this specification, not all.

## Current best-supported positioning
A model-specific study of what finite observations can establish about adaptive feedback,
what stronger truthful disclosure adds, and why neither is sufficient for operational financial
safety. A paradigm-forming result would require external evidence and research uptake; this
package does not promise or self-certify that outcome.
